We believe the building mechanic will work, and if it doesn't, the project should pivot to be focused on just the platforming part, without levels where the player needs to build
to complete the level.

The building is the basis of the game, so if it doesn't work the entire game premise would probably not work; though perhaps we could pivot to a basic platformer.

The pass condition is that you are able to use your mouse to place down the different materials on the level, and move them around. Locking their position once the level/actual 
platforming starts isn't really needed for now. It would be good to also make it so that you can't place the building materials inside of each other.
