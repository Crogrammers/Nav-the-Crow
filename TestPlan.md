# The script for a session: what you tell testers beforehand (keep it short), the tasks you ask them to do, so they're ready for the post-play questions.

The goal is to get to the wallet (gray hexagon)

You can collect coins (yellow hexagons) though it is not strictly necessary.

A/D or arrow keys to move player, or move camera in build mode.

Space to jump.

In build mode, left click to create objects and move them around, right click to destroy an object.

When you finish building, press "Exit Build Mode"

You will now be in platforming mode.

Get to the wallet as fast as you can!

While playing, please think about how easy the building system is to understand and how the player movement feels.

Fill out this form when finished playing: https://forms.gle/Ze4nesnjt6rcpcHU6 (if you'd like you can take a look at it before playing)
